How your details are used

Christmas Planner by @itsgemma__. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who looks after your details

Gemma (@itsgemma__) runs this planner. If you have any question about your details, email itsgemma.hello@gmail.com (copy it if the link does nothing).

What we keep

  • Your first name and email address, so we can send you your personal link and know whose planner is whose.
  • What you type into your planner: the names of the people you're buying for, their budgets, the presents, prices, links, and your ordered and arrived ticks.
  • When your planner was last opened.

Only you can see your planner. Use first names or nicknames for the people on your list; there's no need to put anything else about them.

Emails

We'll always email you your personal link, because that's how you open your planner on another device. You'll only get Gem's other emails if you ticked the box to say yes. Every one of those has an unsubscribe link.

Where it's stored

Your planner is stored with Netlify, who host this website. Your name and email are also held by Klaviyo, who send the emails. Both keep data securely and only use it to provide their service to us.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in on this device for up to a year. It's needed for the planner to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. If a planner hasn't been opened for two years, we'll delete it.

Deleting everything

Open your planner and tap Delete my planner at the bottom. That deletes your planner and your details straight away, and asks Klaviyo to delete your email too. Or email us and we'll do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
